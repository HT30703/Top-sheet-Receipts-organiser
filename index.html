How to use this tool

Reads any PDF, shows real page thumbnails, auto-matches receipts to topsheets by reading their text, and exports one print-ready PDF plus a Sage import CSV. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Step 1 · Build sequence

  1. Paste your credit card statement (whole block, headers and all) on the left — the master order.
  2. Paste your AutoEntry export (with header row) on the right. Columns used: SupplierDescription, BaseCurrencyTotal, Description, AccountCode, AccountCodeDescription, Reference, InvoiceDate.
  3. Click Order export to statement. Refunds are excluded automatically; amounts are auto-corrected against AutoEntry.

Step 2 · Load PDFs

Drop your topsheets PDF, then drop all your individual receipt PDFs at once. Real page thumbnails render for both.

Step 3 · Match & order

Click Auto-match by text — it reads payee/amount off each receipt and pairs it to the right topsheet. Fix any by dragging a receipt thumbnail onto a topsheet. Low-confidence matches are outlined amber.

Step 4 · Export

Download the merged PDF (topsheet + its receipts, in order) and the Sage import CSV. Both share one order.

Tips & what the colours mean
Green = confident (receipt total matches, and the payee name or a unique amount confirms it). Amber = matched but worth a glance. Uncertain ones are left unmatched rather than guessed. Click a matched card to verify side-by-side; ✓ Confirm locks it in. Use ↕ Sort to statement order to arrange the print pack, then reorder any card by dragging its ⠿ handle (or the ▲▼ / number).
